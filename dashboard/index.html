Methodology and boundaries

Evidence first. Every package and standalone item links to official source records. FedPulse classifies metadata; it does not read or summarize full text.

Separate clocks. FR daily totals, complete-week FR activity/level metrics, MARC cataloged-date horizon, and source freshness are not interchangeable.

Confidence is a gate. Unknown agencies, missing URLs, concentrated MARC batches, and weak samples lower confidence. Low-confidence signals are not Telegram/brief candidates.

Lifecycle. New, continuing, stale, and resolved state is persisted. Unchanged continuing signals do not notify merely because a cooldown elapsed; material changes and transitions do.
